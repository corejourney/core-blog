Today the standard for being an authority on knowledge is a committment to being unbiased, to put it another way, not believing in anything at all. They proclaim with ironic certainty that, "I do not have an ideology, a party or interests that I support, nor a dogmatic philosophy! Only to the cold hard facts, and what *actually works*".

Putting aside the pressing question of how one actually determines what constitutes a "cold hard fact" or what a working system, which of course is a philosophical question, this viewpoint is a manifestion of something extremely real in society. It is a call to ignore the functioning of society by affirming that it cannot be understood as a whole in the first place, in favour for focusing on narrow personal aims. It is an idea that denies change outright.
